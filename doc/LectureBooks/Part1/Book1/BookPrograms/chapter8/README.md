# Programs for chapter 8

Every number, table and figure of chapter 8 (resolution refinement, the rodeo algorithm, the comparison with VQE and phase estimation, the four model Hamiltonians, and the gate budget) is produced by the programs in this folder, on top of the shared modules (`../common/gates.py`, `circuit.py`, `fockspace.py`, `models.py`, `pauli.py`, `jordanwigner.py`, `matrixfunctions.py`, `optimize.py`, `bookplot.py`), `coupledcluster.py` of chapter 3, `mapped_models.py` and `pauli_circuits.py` of chapter 4, and `phase_estimation.py` and `qpe_models.py` of chapter 6. Keep this list and the `Programs for this chapter` section of `chapter8.tex` in sync. Figures are written to `../../BookFigures/chapter8/`.

| Program | What it computes | Runtime |
|---|---|---|
| `refinement.py` | Classes `Prolongation` (`embedding`: new levels empty, gate-free; `orbital`: a_c^† → Σ_f W_fc a_f^†, determinants of minors, Eq. 8.prolongdet) and `ResolutionRefinement` (path Hamiltonian of Eqs. 8.path / 8.pathlattice with `match_energy=True`, spectrum and minimum gap along the path, evolution by exact midpoint slices or by a supplied product-formula `stepper`, overlap, energy, `time_to_overlap`); `pairing_refinement(k_low, k_high, N, g, buffer, psi_low)`. As a script: pairing k = 2 → 4 for N = 2, 4 (Fig. 8.refine), four particles to k = 3, 4 (Fig. 8.fourparticle), comparison with a linear coupling ramp | ~10 s |
| `rodeo.py` | Class `RodeoFilter` (`cycle_ancilla`: the one-ancilla circuit on the joint register; `cycle`: the filter (1 + e^{iEt} e^{-iHt})/2; `all_zero_probability`, `scan`, `expected` (exact Gaussian averages, Eq. 8.PM), `prepare` (sampled time sets, acceptance-weighted), `run` (sampled ancilla outcomes); optional `evolution(t)` for product formulas), `gaussian_factor`, `infidelity_bound`. As a script: checks of Eqs. 8.cos2–8.gaussian, the one-qubit tapered pairing Hamiltonian (Fig. 8.onequbit), sampled runs | ~10 s |
| `uccsd_sector.py` | Class `UCCSDSector`: product-form UCCSD in an N-particle sector (generators as index arrays, exp(θA) = 1 + sin θ A + (1 − cos θ) A², exact gradient by a backward sweep, BFGS from θ = 0); `pairing_references(k)`: FCI, CCD, UCCSD. Table 8.uccsd and Fig. 8.uccsd for k = 2…8 | ~5 s |
| `pairing_pipeline.py` | Chains k = 2 → 4 and k = 3 (UCCSD) → 4 (Fig. 8.chains); coupling scan and buffer scan, T* against the inverse gap (Fig. 8.gapscan); rodeo scans σ = 2 and σ = 8 (Fig. 8.scan); infidelity and acceptance against M for the refined and prolonged inputs (Table 8.pipeline, Fig. 8.convergence); three cycles with times π/(E_j − E_0) | ~30 s |
| `filters_compared.py` | Class `PhaseEstimationFilter` (phase estimation as a filter through the Dirichlet amplitude) against the rodeo filter at equal total evolution time (Table 8.qpevsrodeo, Fig. 8.qpevsrodeo); energy error and infidelity of the k = 4 UCCSD state and rodeo cycles applied to it | ~2 s |
| `other_models.py` | Refinement hierarchies for the Lipkin model (N = 2 → 4, embedding), the Heisenberg ring (2 → 4 and 4 → 8 sites, singlet covering = magnon prolongation with the Marshall sign) and the Hubbard ring (2 → 4 sites, bonding orbitals); Tables 8.othermodels and 8.otherrodeo, Fig. 8.othermodels; two particles on an open chain refined 2 → 4 → 8 sites with t → 4t, U → 2U | ~10 s |
| `trotter_pipeline.py` | Class `PairingSplit` (H = D + Σ h_pq in the sector, first- and second-order steps, fixed-step-size evolution); product-formula error; Trotterised refinement and Trotterised rodeo filter (Fig. 8.trotter, Table 8.trotterrodeo); ground state of the effective Hamiltonian of one step | ~15 s |
| `gate_budget.py` | Pauli-rotation circuit check; Jordan–Wigner strings and CNOT counts of single/double excitations and of the UCCSD preparation (272 at k = 3, 1312 at k = 4); Trotter-step counts 24k(k−1)+2k, plain and controlled, Jordan–Wigner against compact encoding; the projector of the path; Table 8.depth; step counts of the four models of Table 6.models; Table 8.faults | ~1 s |

Conventions: levels are labelled from 0, mode = qubit = 2·level + spin (the ordering of `PairingModel`); all refinement and rodeo calculations act in a number (or S^z) sector with dense matrices; the rodeo phase gate is `P(Et) = diag(1, e^{iEt})` on the ancilla, which is the first tensor factor; Gaussian times have zero mean and width σ (negative times are allowed, the filter depends on |t| only through cos²); infidelities of "accepted runs" are acceptance-weighted averages over time sets; T* is the first integer T at which the overlap reaches 0.99 and stays there for the next three grid points; refinement uses 160 midpoint slices unless stated. All programs run on `numpy` and `matplotlib` only; each runs as a script with `assert`-based checks.
