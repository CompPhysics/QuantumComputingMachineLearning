# Programs for chapter 7

Every number, table and figure of chapter 7 is produced by the programs in this folder, on top of the shared modules of chapters 2–4 (`../common/statevector.py`, `circuit.py`, `gates.py`, `pauli.py`, `models.py`, `optimize.py`, `jordanwigner.py`), the chapter-4 programs `mapped_models.py` and `pauli_circuits.py` (measurement circuits, `energy_from_shots`, the pair-excitation gate) and the chapter-6 program `qpe_models.py` (the four model Hamiltonians and their sector ground states). Keep this list and the `Programs for this chapter` section of `chapter7.tex` in sync. Figures are written to `../../BookFigures/chapter7/`.

| Program | What it computes | Runtime |
|---|---|---|
| `vqe.py` | The ansatz classes `HardwareEfficient` (R_y R_z layers + CNOT ladder or ring), `ProductState` (mean field) and `PauliRotationAnsatz` (reference state followed by exp(−iθ_k G_k) for Hermitian generators: UCC, Hamiltonian-variational and ADAPT circuits); the class `VQE` with exact or shot-sampled energies, the two-term and four-term parameter-shift gradients, gradient descent, BFGS and SPSA; `run_restarts`. As a script: checks against the chapter-4 circuits and finite differences, the three optimisers on the two-mode Hamiltonian | ~2 s |
| `measurement.py` | The rotation recipe on a random state (Table 7.rotationcheck), shot noise of a fixed trial state (Table 7.shots, Fig. 7.shotnoise), the marginal trap: Bell-state counterexample, the flat and the fictitious landscape (Fig. 7.fakelandscape), a VQE run with marginal read-out on the two-level pairing Hamiltonian (Table 7.marginal) | ~10 s |
| `gradients.py` | The trigonometric form of the energy in one angle, the two-term rule against finite differences, its failure for the generator (Z_0+Z_1)/2 and the four-term rule (`general_shift`), the barren plateau of the hardware-efficient ansatz with L = n layers (Fig. 7.barren), SPSA against parameter-shift descent on the pairing Hamiltonian (Fig. 7.spsa) | ~15 s |
| `vqe_models.py` | VQE on the tapered one-qubit and the two-mode Hamiltonians (Table 7.small); the four models of chapters 4 and 6 with the hardware-efficient ansatz at increasing depth (Table 7.hea) and with ansätze that know the physics — mean field and Hamiltonian-variational for Lipkin, the pair-excitation circuit and all six pair transfers for pairing, the Hamiltonian-variational ansatz from the weak-coupling reference for the Hubbard ring and from the singlet covering for the Heisenberg ring (Table 7.physics, Fig. 7.convergence); overlaps with the exact ground state and the comparison with phase estimation (Table 7.qpevsvqe) | ~70 s |
| `adapt_vqe.py` | The class `AdaptVQE` (pool gradients i⟨[G,H]⟩, growth by the largest gradient, re-optimisation), the pools (`lipkin_pools`, `heisenberg_pool`, `hubbard_pool`) and the runs of Table 7.adapt and Fig. 7.adapt, including the gradient trap of the two-operator collective Lipkin pool | ~15 s |
| `qaoa.py` | `qubo_to_ising`, `ising_hamiltonian`, `maxcut_hamiltonian`, the class `QAOA` (phase separator, R_x mixer, BFGS with restarts); the QUBO example (Table 7.qubo), the p = 1 closed forms for one edge and for the ring, MaxCut on the four-vertex ring and the six-vertex prism graph for p = 1, 2, 3 (Table 7.qaoa), the p = 1 landscape (Fig. 7.qaoalandscape) and the output distributions (Fig. 7.qaoa) | ~5 s |

Conventions: qubit 0 is the leftmost tensor factor and `|1>` is occupied, as in chapter 4; `PauliRotationAnsatz` applies its generators in the order given (the first acts first on the reference) with `exp(-i theta G)`, so the pair-excitation gate of Eq. (4.pairgadget) is the generator `-(1/2)(Y_i X_a - X_i Y_a)`; `QAOA.state` uses `exp(-i gamma H_C)` followed by `R_x(2 beta)` on every qubit, i.e. `exp(-i beta X_q)`. All programs run on `numpy` and `matplotlib` only; each runs as a script with `assert`-based checks.
